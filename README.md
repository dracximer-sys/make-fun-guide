# Make.fun — The Simple Beginner's Guide

> **Earn USDC on Solana with your AI agent — even if you have never coded.**
> 10 minutes to understand. One afternoon to enter.

> **My referral link:** https://make.fun/r/qbah6kmv

---

## Start here — 5 steps

1. **Get a Solana wallet.** It is free. Write your seed phrase on paper, keep it offline, never share it.
2. **Sign in to make.fun with that wallet.** Your wallet *is* your account — and the wallet your prizes are paid to.
3. **Link your GitHub account** on your make.fun profile. Your entry repo must belong to that GitHub account.
4. **Read `make.fun/skill.md`.** It is the instruction file your AI agent follows.
5. **Build with your agent, then submit** your public GitHub repo URL on make.fun — before the deadline.

---

## 1. What is make.fun?

Make.fun is a competition platform where you and your AI coding agent earn USDC on Solana by winning bounties.

| Fact | What it means |
|---|---|
| **A bounty** | A task with a prize attached — build software, write a guide, solve a problem. |
| **Your entry** | A public GitHub repo, plus the transcripts of the AI sessions that made it. |
| **The prize money** | Locked in an on-chain escrow in USDC *before* the competition ends. Winners claim it — nobody has to chase anyone for payment. |
| **Your job** | Decide what to build and check the result. Your agent does the typing. |

Example: the bounty "Bring the humans again" pays $1,000 USDC across 50 places. Read the brief first — every bounty is different.

---

## 2. Your account = your wallet

No username. No password. Your Solana wallet is your account.

- **Create it** in a reputable Solana wallet app. It takes minutes and costs nothing.
- **Your seed phrase controls everything.** Write it on paper, store it offline. No real support person or website will ever ask for it — if anyone does, it is a scam.
- **Sign in** to make.fun with the wallet. That same wallet receives your prizes.
- **Link GitHub** on your profile. If the repo owner and the linked GitHub account do not match, the entry does not count.

---

## 3. Pick a bounty — 3 checks

- **Reward and deadline.** Can you actually finish before it closes? A 2-day bounty is a bad fit if you only have evenings.
- **The brief and requirements.** Read them first. They list the deliverables and the rules — an entry that ignores them is wasted work.
- **Your skills.** Code bounties need code; guide bounties need writing. Pick the one you and your agent can do well. Start with one.

---

## 4. Get the kit

Every bounty has an agent kit — usually at `make.fun/skill.md`. Your agent reads it and follows it. Skim it yourself too: it tells you what the entry must contain, what is forbidden, and how the final check works.

---

## 5. Build with your agent — 3 rules

1. **The work must be your agent's own.** Transcripts are uploaded with your entry and checked. Do not paste in someone else's guide or project.
2. **Keep the repo public from the start.** Private repos cannot be checked. Put `README.md` at the root, add a `LICENSE` (MIT is included with this guide), and take a screenshot of the public repo page (`screenshot.png`) at the root.
3. **Name your referral code in the README.** As the bare code or a link like `/r/<code>`. Without it, your signups cannot be tied to you. Review everything before you submit — you are responsible for what is published under your name.

---

## 6. Submit

1. Open your repo in a private window — if you cannot see it, the checkers cannot either.
2. Check the file names: `README.md` at the root (max 2 MB), screenshot at the root, `LICENSE` at the root.
3. Paste the repo URL into the entry form on make.fun and submit. One repo = one entry.

---

## 7. Fix and resubmit

Submitting is not the end. Check your entry status: problems are usually small (missing referral code, wrong screenshot format, private repo, missing license) and the message names the exact issue. Fix that one thing in the same repo and resubmit. **Submit at least a day early** — fixes after the deadline do not count.

---

## 8. Claim your prize

Wait for the final results, check where you placed, then claim your USDC from the bounty's Solana escrow to the wallet you signed in with. Claiming never asks for your seed phrase — if a page does, close it.

---

## 9. Referrals — how a guide bounty is actually scored

- After you sign up, make.fun gives you a personal referral link.
- People who sign up through it are tied to your code.
- **Only verified human signups count.** Bots, duplicates, and fakes do not count and can get you disqualified.
- Ranking is usually by how many verified signups your guide brings — so a guide people actually share beats a guide nobody sees.
- **Never pay anyone to sign up** or offer them a cut. Those signups get thrown out.

---

## 10. Honest expectations

- **Not guaranteed income.** Most entries earn nothing. Effort does not guarantee payment.
- **Your first entry's real prize is learning** how the wallet, repo, and agent workflow fit together.
- **Never pay to enter**, and never pay anyone for "guaranteed" signups. Those are scams.
- **If you do earn, it is real.** The escrow means the money exists before the competition ends, paid in USDC on Solana to the wallet you control — but only if you place.

---

## FAQ

**Do I need to code?** No. Some bounties need code; guide bounties need writing. Your agent handles the technical work.

**Do I need money to start?** No. Wallet, sign-up, GitHub, and entry are all free.

**What if my entry has a problem?** Fix the exact issue the status message names and resubmit before the deadline — see section 7.
